## Context

This is the part drivers hold in their hands: a **Flutter companion app** (owner's manual, AI chat, alerts, maintenance plans, dealer locator) and the **.NET mobile BFF** behind it. The BFF shapes data for each screen, so the app never talks to internal services directly. The [chatbot](/projects/automotive-rag-chatbot) and the [content platform](/projects/automotive-content-platform) have their own case studies.

## Key decisions

### Privacy that holds up in an audit
- **Consent is a record, not a checkbox.** Drivers consent at first login and can change it in Settings → Privacy. Every accept and withdraw is stored **append-only**, so the history can't be rewritten.
- **The privacy text matches the app's language.** Privacy and terms pages exist in English, French, Spanish and Japanese.
- **Right to erasure (GDPR Art. 17)** follows a written runbook that covers every table in every service.

### A driver's data follows the driver
Early builds saved chat history, consent and maintenance plans per phone. When accounts arrived, a signed-in driver would have seen none of it. I moved ownership to the account and wrote a one-time migration that is safe to run:
- **All or nothing:** one transaction, on a single instance, switched on by ops.
- **Resumable:** progress is saved, so a crash picks up where it stopped.
- **Self-isolating:** if a batch fails, it splits in half to find the bad row.
- **Memory too:** the chatbot's conversation memory is relinked in batches of 500.

### Weather tips that never show an error
The Tips tab shows AI driving tips for the current weather, written from the car's own manual.
- **Coordinates never leave the phone.** The app sends only a coarse bucket, such as "heavy rain, cold".
- **Drivers share answers.** One cached answer serves every driver of the same car, language and weather.
- **Fails quietly.** It survives a Redis outage and respects upstream rate limits, and each paid generation's cost shows up on the admin dashboard.

### Help when the AI can't answer
When the chatbot can't solve a question, the app offers support. The case it sends **includes the conversation and the reason** it went unresolved, so the support agent doesn't start from zero.

### Per-region settings, no app release
Dealer-locator links, disclaimers and manual sources are set per region in the admin panel and pushed to the BFF. Each change is a new versioned shape, so **older app versions keep working**.

## Results

- **1,500+ automated tests** on the BFF.
- **Every driver-facing feature** served through one backend.
- **Consent you can audit** record by record, and erasure by runbook.
- **4 languages** for legal pages.

## What I'd do differently

Make the account own driver data from day one, even before sign-up existed. Fixing it later cost a migration, a memory relink across two services, and an extra erasure runbook.
